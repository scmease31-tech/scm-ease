Scope — driven by the Store Stock Report: every item whose Category/Sub-Category is Packing (any sub-category), Raw → Backlabel, Raw → Barcode & Logo (UL and Non-UL both included), or Raw → Solar RFID, or Consumable → Module Consumables — whether or not that item also has a row in the Minimum Store Stock file. Items with no minimum-file record show a "no min set" tag and default to 0.
Combined stock — quantity summed across every location, excluding any location code containing "REJ" (covers REJE U-2 and WH1 U2 REJ style reject lots).
Editable — Min. Qty. Required and Combined Stock cells can be overridden by hand (marked with an amber dot); Difference recalculates instantly. Export writes live formulas for every cell you haven't touched by hand, and your typed value for the ones you have.
Row detail — click any row (outside the editable boxes) to expand its variant-wise breakdown: quantity by Variant Code/Name and location, with reject lots flagged separately.
